50s
1950s

The Age of Titans

Room-sized machines of vacuum tubes that could perform calculations no human ever could.

  • ENIAC weighed 30 tons and used 18,000 vacuum tubes.
  • IBM's first commercial computer launched in 1952.
  • FORTRAN — the first high-level language — was born.

Vacuum Tube Era
60s
1960s

The Space Age

Integrated circuits shrank the computer. NASA used them to reach the Moon.

  • The Apollo Guidance Computer ran on just 4KB of RAM.
  • Doug Engelbart invented the mouse and hypertext.
  • ARPANET — the Internet's ancestor — went live in 1969.

1960
Space Age Computing
70s
1970s

The Spark of the Microchip

An entire CPU on a single chip. The personal computer was no longer a dream.

  • Intel's 4004 — the first microprocessor — had 2,300 transistors.
  • Apple was founded in a garage in 1976.
  • Microsoft was born writing BASIC for the Altair 8800.
Explore the 1970s ›
1970
Microprocessor Era
80s
1980s

The Dawn of the Personal Computer

Computers moved from the lab to the living room. Icons replaced commands.

  • IBM PC (1981) created the entire PC industry.
  • Apple Macintosh (1984) introduced the mouse-driven GUI.
  • Microsoft Windows 1.0 launched in 1985.
Explore the 1980s ›
1980
Personal Computer Era
90s
1990s

The Rise of the Internet

The World Wide Web connected humanity. The planet shrank overnight.

  • Tim Berners-Lee launched the Web publicly in 1991.
  • Windows 95 brought millions online for the first time.
  • Google was founded in a dorm room in 1998.
Explore the 1990s ›
1990
Internet Era
00s
2000s

The Digital Millennium

Social media, smartphones, and the cloud put computing in every pocket.

  • Facebook, YouTube, and Twitter all launched this decade.
  • The iPhone (2007) redefined what a phone could be.
  • Amazon AWS started the cloud computing revolution.
Explore the 2000s ›
2000
Digital Millennium
10s
2010s

The Age of Intelligence

Machines learned to see, hear, and understand. AI stopped being science fiction.

  • Deep learning achieved human-level image recognition by 2012.
  • Siri, Alexa, and Google Assistant entered everyday life.
  • AlphaGo defeated the world's best Go player in 2016.
