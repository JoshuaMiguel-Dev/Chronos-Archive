◆ The Architects ◆

About the Project

Chronos Archive is an educational website created by four first-year Computer Science students at the University of Caloocan City, dedicated to preserving and presenting the history of computing for future generations.

◆ The Mission ◆

Why We Built This

The Purpose of Chronos Archive

We are CS students who believe that understanding where computing came from is essential to understanding where it is going. The history of computing is filled with remarkable human stories — of inventors working in garages, mathematicians cracking unbreakable codes, and visionaries who saw the future decades before anyone else.

Chronos Archive is named after the Greek god of time — a fitting tribute for a website that traces the journey of computing across seven decades. Just as Chronos governs the flow of time, this archive documents the moments that shaped the modern digital world.

Our Objectives

What We Set Out to Do

✦

Show how computers transformed from room-sized machines to personal devices in the span of one human lifetime.

✦

Present the key inventions, milestones, and personalities that drove each decade's breakthroughs.

✦

Trace the evolution of programming languages from assembly to Python and beyond.

✦

Celebrate the pioneers — many of them overlooked — who built the foundation of modern computing.

✦

Provide an engaging, visually rich learning experience for students, educators, and curious minds.

◆ Limitations ◆

Honest Disclosure

What This Project Cannot Do

✦

Content Accuracy Depends on Available Sources — The historical information presented is based only on publicly available references and has not been verified by a subject matter expert or professional historian.

✦

Pioneer Selection is Subjective — The individuals featured on the Key Figures page were chosen based on the group's research and judgment. Some notable contributors may have been unintentionally left out.

✦

Content Presentation is One-Directional — The website only delivers information with no way for visitors to give feedback or report errors, as there is no comment section or contact form available.
